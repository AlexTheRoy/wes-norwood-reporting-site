# Wes Norwood Insurance Agency Reporting Site

Static client reporting site for Wes Norwood Insurance Agency LLC, Allstate (GOAL account #777), deployed on Vercel.

- `index.html`: hub with Campaign Reports and Website & Research
- `Wes-Norwood-*-Since-Launch-10-19-26.html`: campaign reports, Feb 10 through Oct 19, 2026
- `Wes-Norwood-Inbound-Call-Review-10-19-26.html`: inbound calls, Jul 21 to Oct 19, 2026
- `Wes-Norwood-Source-ZIP-Sales-10-19-26.html` and `Wes-Norwood-ZIP-Sales-10-19-26.csv`: quotes and sales by source, county and ZIP
- `Wes-Norwood-SEO-Audit-10-19-26.html`, `Wes-Norwood-TX-*`: website audit and Texas research
- `vercel.json`: clean URLs, security headers, noindex

No build step. Push to `main` and Vercel redeploys.

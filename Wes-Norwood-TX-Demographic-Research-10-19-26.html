Demographic profile of all 1,923 populated Texas ZIP codes: household income, homeownership, home values, age and vehicles, built from the U.S. Census Bureau's ACS 2020–2024 five-year estimates (released December 2025), the most current ZIP-level data published. Dallas-Fort Worth, where about a third of your leads come from, is called out throughout: the four core counties (Dallas, Tarrant, Collin and Denton), which alone supply about a quarter. Bexar County (San Antonio), your second-largest lead county after Dallas, is the second market. Section 07 lines the state up against the 3,992 GOAL leads delivered from February 10 to October 19, 2026, about eight months of leads across all four campaigns (2,726 Home and 1,266 Auto). The rest of your leads are spread across West Texas (El Paso first), Central Texas, East Texas and Houston. The companion spreadsheet carries every ZIP and every column; the hail and hurricane report covers the risk geography.

Data & files

  • SourceU.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey 2020–2024 five-year estimates (released December 2025), tables B01002, B01003, B08201, B11001, B11007, B19013, B25001, B25002, B25003, B25004, B25077. Geography: 2020 ZIP Code Tabulation Areas.
  • Coverage1,923 populated Texas ZIPs. PO-box-only ZIPs have no resident population and no Census profile; traffic from them resolves to the surrounding ZIP. Median income is published for 1,746 ZIPs and median home value for 1,701.
  • Top-codingThe Census caps medians at “$250,000 or more” for income (2 ZIPs) and “$2,000,000 or more” for home value (0 ZIPs); those ZIPs are shown at the cap.
  • GeographyEach ZIP is assigned to the Texas county holding the largest share of its land (2020 ZCTA-to-county relationship file); ZIPs in the 75 to 79 and 885 ranges that sit outside Texas are left out. The 254 counties are grouped into eight regions: Houston & Gulf Coast, the Golden Triangle (Beaumont and Port Arthur), the Rio Grande Valley, San Antonio & South Texas (including Corpus Christi and Laredo), Austin & Central Texas, Dallas-Fort Worth, East Texas, and West Texas & Panhandle (including El Paso). The city column is left blank: no city file was used, so ZIPs are identified by county.
  • LeadsAll 3,992 GOAL leads for this account across all four campaigns (two Home, two Auto), Feb 10 to Oct 19, 2026, about eight months of leads (2,726 Home, 1,266 Auto), counted by the ZIP on the lead. 2,720 of 2,726 Home leads and 1,258 of 1,266 Auto leads matched a populated Texas ZIP. 6 Home and 8 Auto leads came from PO-box or non-residential ZIPs (75267, 76195, 77225, 77243, 77250, 77413, 78074, 78288, 78627, 78646, 79409, 79926, 95747) with no Census profile.
  • PrecisionAll values carry ACS margins of error and are strongest for larger ZIPs. Blank means not published, never zero.
  • FilesWes-Norwood-TX-ZIP-Data-10-19-26.csv · every ZIP, every column, with the hail and hurricane tier from the companion report.
